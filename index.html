How It Works

Up and running without a project

No agents on endpoints. Nothing for you to run. One small collector, an encrypted tunnel, then we start paying attention.

1

We talk

How the office actually runs. What is normal, who to call, what not to panic about. The relationship starts here — not with a scan.

2

We connect

A lightweight collector on site (Raspberry Pi or a small Ubuntu VM). Your firewall sends traffic metadata to it locally; the collector forwards that to us over WireGuard. Never as a direct feed to the open internet.

3

We pay attention

Most mornings: a short note. When something isn't ordinary: a human look, in context, before we bother you. The analysis stays on our side.
