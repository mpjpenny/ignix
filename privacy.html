Privacy

This page covers ignix.co.uk, the public site. It is a set of static pages. There is no account, no shop, and no form that stores what you type.

If you email us

Mail to hello@ignix.co.uk is read by Ignix so we can reply. We keep that correspondence for as long as the conversation, or the work that follows from it, is live.

Visits to this site

Pages are counted with Umami, hosted by us at analytics.ignix.co.uk. It records a page view: the page, a referrer, the browser, and the country. It does not use advertising cookies and it does not build a profile of you for anyone else.

The monitoring service

If Ignix watches a network for you, that work is a separate agreement. Traffic metadata from your firewall is not collected by this website, and it is not described here.
